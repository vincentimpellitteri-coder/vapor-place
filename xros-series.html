What is SMT?

Surface Mount Technology (SMT) enables high-quality, durable electronics by mounting components directly onto PCB surfaces via automated, precise reflow soldering. This technique improves mechanical reliability, boosts performance with smaller components, and allows for higher-density, compact designs that are more resistant to vibration and thermal stress compared to traditional methods — making it ideal for a device you carry every day.
