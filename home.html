How Do I Pick a Nicotine Strength?

⌃

Nicotine strength is measured in milligrams (mg). Higher mg = stronger hit. Choosing the wrong strength is the most common beginner mistake — too low and you won't feel satisfied, too high and it'll be harsh.

0mgNo nicotine
3mgVery light — casual or social smoker
6mgLight smoker
25mg SaltHalf-pack-a-day smoker
50mg SaltPack-a-day+ smoker

A general rule: if you smoked a pack a day or more, start with 25–50mg salt nic. Light or social smokers do better with 3–6mg freebase in a larger device. Still unsure? Ask us — we help people figure this out every day.

Salt Nic vs. Regular E-Liquid — What's the Difference?

⌃

There are two main types of e-liquid nicotine: Salt Nic and Freebase (Sub-Ohm). They're not interchangeable — each is designed for a specific type of device.

Salt Nic (30ml)

  • Smooth even at high strengths (25–50mg)
  • Fast nicotine absorption — satisfies cravings quickly
  • Used in pod systems & small devices
  • Great for smokers switching to vaping
  • Less vapor, more discreet

Freebase / Sub-Ohm (60–100ml)

  • Lower nicotine (3–12mg) — smoother at low doses
  • Produces large, dense clouds
  • Used in box mods & sub-ohm tanks
  • Better for experienced vapers
  • Stronger, more intense flavor

Bottom line: If you're new, start with Salt Nic in a pod device. If you're chasing clouds or already vaping, sub-ohm may be for you. Using the wrong liquid in the wrong device won't give you the experience you're looking for — and we're always here to help you match them correctly.

Understanding Nicotine Labels — mg vs. %

⌃

Nicotine strength is shown two ways depending on the brand — milligrams (mg) or percentage (%). They mean the exact same thing, just written differently.

The conversion is simple: divide mg by 10 to get %.

3mg= 0.3%
6mg= 0.6%
12mg= 1.2%
25mg= 2.5%
30mg= 3.0%
50mg= 5.0%

So if one bottle says 50mg and another says 5% — they're the same strength. Some brands (especially disposables) switched to percentage labeling in recent years, which is why you'll see both formats in our store. When in doubt, just ask — we'll make sure you leave with the right strength.
