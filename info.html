Know Your Vape

Quick answers to common questions — no tech degree required.

Salt Nic vs. Regular Nicotine

🧂

Nicotine Salts (Salt Nic)

Salt nic uses a smoother form of nicotine that absorbs quickly — similar to how a cigarette delivers nicotine. It comes in higher strengths (25mg–50mg) and is best used in low-power pod devices. Great for people transitioning from cigarettes or who want a strong hit without a harsh throat feel.

💨

Regular (Freebase) Nicotine

The traditional form of nicotine used in most e-liquids. It can feel harsher at high strengths, so it's usually sold at lower levels (3mg–12mg). It's best suited for sub-ohm tanks and box mods that produce bigger clouds. Ideal for experienced vapers who prefer a direct-to-lung style.

💡 Simple rule: Pod device? Use salt nic. Box mod with a big tank? Use regular nic. Not sure? Ask us.

Device Care Tips

🔧

Keep It Clean

Wipe down your device regularly, especially around the pod or tank connection. A cotton swab works great. E-liquid buildup causes poor connections and muted flavor.

💧

Prime Your Coils

When you install a new coil, add a few drops of e-liquid directly onto the cotton wick and let it sit for 5–10 minutes before vaping. This prevents a nasty burnt hit and extends coil life.

📦

Store It Right

Keep your device upright when not in use to prevent leaking. Avoid leaving it in a hot car or in direct sunlight — heat degrades the coil and battery faster.

🔋

Charge Smart

Don't leave your device charging overnight or unattended for long periods. Charge it to full, then unplug it. Most devices are fine with the included cable, but use a quality USB charger — cheap chargers can damage batteries.

Battery Safety

⚠️

Never Use Damaged Batteries

If your battery has a torn wrap (the thin plastic sleeve), stop using it immediately. A torn wrap can cause a short circuit. We can rewrap batteries in-store — just bring it in.

🚗

Don't Leave Batteries in Your Car

Extreme heat is the #1 enemy of lithium batteries. A hot car in summer can push a battery past its safe temperature range. Store loose batteries in a case — never loose in a pocket or bag with coins or keys.

🔌

Use the Right Charger

Always charge with a charger rated for your battery. If your mod has a built-in USB-C port, that's fine for everyday use. For external batteries, a dedicated battery charger is the safest option.

♻️

Dispose Properly

Never throw lithium batteries in the trash. Bring your old batteries to either of our locations and we'll dispose of them safely and responsibly for you — no questions asked.

What Do Battery Numbers Mean?

Battery names like 18650 or 21700 aren't random — they describe the physical size of the battery.

📏

How to Read the Number

The digits represent diameter × height in millimeters. So an 18650 is 18mm wide and 65mm tall. A 21700 is 21mm wide and 70mm tall. That's it.

🔋

18650 — The Classic

The most common vape battery. Widely available, reliable, and used in a huge range of mods. A solid all-around choice that's been the industry standard for years.

⚡

21700 — The Upgrade

Slightly larger, which means more capacity (longer battery life) and often higher power output. Many newer mods are built around this size. If your device takes either, the 21700 is usually the better pick.

🔍

What to Look For

Beyond size, look for high drain ratings (the amperage, labeled CDR or A). For most vaping, a battery rated at 20A or higher is plenty. Stick to reputable brands — Samsung, Sony, LG, Molicel. Avoid off-brand batteries with no specs listed.
